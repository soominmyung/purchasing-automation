## Findings

F1. NordicFasteners — shipments were held for four weeks in November 2024 due to a customs documentation error at Gothenburg. The supplier has since changed its export paperwork preparation. [1]
F2. HexBoltM12 — runs out before a new order can arrive; the gap is 9 weeks. (stock position)
F3. HexBoltM12 — no documents on this item.
F4. WasherSteel — demand has risen by about 60% since January 2025 due to a new construction customer. [2]
F5. AnchorPlug — A batch of AnchorPlug delivered by NordicFasteners in December 2024 had about 3% of units with cracked sleeves. Replacements arrived two weeks later. [3]
F6. CableTie — demand has been steady over the past year. [4]

## Critical Questions

Ask NordicFasteners:
1. Has the customs documentation process change by NordicFasteners addressed the shipment delay issue, and could it affect HexBoltM12, WasherSteel, AnchorPlug, or CableTie in this order? (F1)
Check internally:
2. Is the increased WasherSteel demand expected to continue with the new construction customer? (F4)

## Proposals

No changes proposed.

## Recommended Actions

1. Ask NordicFasteners for expedited shipping of HexBoltM12, or cover it from an alternative source, to bridge the 9-week gap. (F2)
2. Confirm delivery dates for this order with NordicFasteners before placing it, given last year's customs delay. (F1)
3. Check whether the WasherSteel order should be larger than suggested, since the suggestion is based on demand before the new customer. (F4)
