## Findings

### From the documents

F1. MeridianSeals — announced that lead times for all products would increase by four weeks from 1 April 2025 while a moulding line is refitted, with the increase expected to last until the end of 2025. [1]
F2. MeridianSeals — shipments were held for three weeks in November 2024 following a customs documentation error at Felixstowe. The supplier says it has since changed how its export paperwork is checked. [2]
F3. OilRing — demand has been steady over the past year. [3]

### From the stock figures (after the proposed changes)

F4. BushingSleeve — has 10 weeks to stockout against a revised lead time of 22 weeks, giving a 12-week gap and a Critical risk level. Its latest order date is 5 April 2025, with the order already overdue.
F5. GasketRing — has 20 weeks to stockout against a revised lead time of 22 weeks, giving a two-week gap and a Critical risk level. Its latest order date is 5 April 2025, with the order already overdue.
F6. ValveCap — has 24 weeks to stockout against a revised lead time of 22 weeks. Its risk level is High, its gap is zero, and its latest order date is 19 April 2025.
F7. SealPlate — has 28 weeks to stockout against a revised lead time of 22 weeks. Its risk level is High, its gap is zero, and its latest order date is 17 May 2025.

## Critical Questions

Ask MeridianSeals:
1. What expedited production, partial shipments or faster transport can you offer for BushingSleeve and GasketRing, and what quantities and delivery dates can you commit to? [F1, F4, F5]
2. What delivery commitments can you provide for the products in this order during the moulding-line refit, and is the announced four-week extension still the expected limit? [F1]
3. What export-document checks now prevent a recurrence of the Felixstowe customs hold, and are any documentation-related delays expected for this order? [F2]

## Proposals

1. Lead time, MeridianSeals overall (expected): ==18 → 22 weeks==: The announced moulding-line refit adds four weeks to the standard total lead time and is expected to affect orders through the end of 2025. The historical customs hold is not added because the supplier reports revised paperwork checks and no continuing delay is documented. [F1, F2]
2. Lead time, BushingSleeve, GasketRing, ValveCap, SealPlate, OilRing: ==18 → 22 weeks==: The supplier's announced refit extension applies to all products and covers the revised ordering dates for these items. There is no documented item-specific exception. The earlier customs hold is not treated as an additional continuing delay because revised paperwork checks have been reported. [F1, F2]

## Recommended Actions

1. Place the BushingSleeve order immediately. In parallel, arrange expedited production or transport, partial deliveries, or alternative-source supply sufficient to bridge the 12-week gap; do not defer the main order while resolving the bridge. [F1, F4]
2. Place the GasketRing order immediately. In parallel, secure an expedited or partial delivery, or alternative-source supply, to bridge the two-week gap without delaying the main order. [F1, F5]
3. Obtain written delivery commitments reflecting the refit extension alongside the immediate BushingSleeve and GasketRing orders, and before placing the remaining orders. Confirm that the revised export-document checks will be applied on the same timetable. [F1, F2]
4. Bring forward the ValveCap order to no later than 19 April 2025 and the SealPlate order to no later than 17 May 2025 to accommodate the refit extension. [F1, F6, F7]
